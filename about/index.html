Built on Cybersecurity Expertise. Designed for Measurable Progress.

iTrust brings TrustNet’s expert-led security testing into a modern platform that helps teams manage findings, remediation, retesting, and evidence of closure.

Help Teams Close the Gap Between Discovery and Remediation

Finding vulnerabilities is essential — but it is only the beginning.

iTrust is designed to help security and engineering teams understand validated risk, prioritize next actions, coordinate remediation, and confirm that fixes work.

Real-World Offensive Security Expertise

TrustNet practitioners bring experience across network, application, API, mobile, cloud, identity, and infrastructure security.

Engagements combine manual testing, attacker-informed techniques, and appropriate offensive tooling to identify weaknesses that automated scanning alone may miss.

The result is practical security intelligence: what can be exploited, why it matters, how to address it, and how to validate closure as environments evolve.

  • Network and infrastructure — internal and external exposure
  • Applications and APIs — authentication, authorization and business logic
  • Mobile — iOS and Android application security
  • Cloud and cloud-native — AWS, Azure and GCP environments
  • Identity — access paths and privilege escalation
