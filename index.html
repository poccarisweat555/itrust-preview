PTaaS, Testing, Remediation and Retesting

What is iTrust?
iTrust is an AI-enabled cybersecurity testing platform that combines expert-led penetration testing with workflows for findings, remediation, retesting, and ongoing visibility. PTaaS is its core offering.
What is Penetration Testing as a Service (PTaaS)?
PTaaS combines expert-led penetration testing with a centralized platform for planning assessments, reviewing findings, managing remediation, requesting retesting, and tracking progress over time.
How is iTrust different from traditional penetration testing?
Traditional engagements often conclude with a static report. iTrust supports the work after discovery by keeping validated findings, remediation activity, retesting, and evidence of closure connected in one workflow.
Does iTrust replace penetration testers with AI?
No. iTrust is expert-led. AI supports remediation guidance and workflow efficiency, while qualified practitioners scope testing, execute assessments, validate findings, and support retesting.
What can iTrust test?
Depending on scope, testing can cover networks and hosts, web applications, APIs, mobile applications, cloud and cloud-native environments, and broader application-layer risks.
Does iTrust replace vulnerability scanners?
No. Scanners can identify potential issues at scale, while expert-led penetration testing validates exploitability, attack paths, and real-world impact. The approaches can complement each other.
How does the AI Remediation Assistant help?
It helps translate validated findings into prioritized, context-aware guidance so security and engineering teams can understand recommended next steps with less back-and-forth.
Can we request retesting after remediation?
Yes. iTrust supports retest requests, validation of implemented fixes, and evidence of closure within the engagement workflow.
How often should penetration testing be performed?
Testing frequency depends on risk, compliance obligations, and how often your environment changes. Many teams test after major releases, infrastructure changes, acquisitions, or remediation—not only on an annual schedule.
How do we get started?
Request a demo and share your testing priorities. The team can walk through the platform and discuss the scope, timeline, and engagement model that fit your environment.
