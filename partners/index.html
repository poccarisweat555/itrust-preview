Frequently Asked Questions

What is the iTrust Partner Program?
The program allows qualified businesses and professionals to refer, position, or resell iTrust to organizations that need penetration testing and structured remediation workflows.
What is the difference between the two programs?
Referral partners introduce qualified opportunities to iTrust. Reseller and technology partners take a more active role in positioning, packaging, selling, or aligning iTrust with their services.
How do referral fees work?
Referral eligibility, fees, payment terms, and opportunity requirements are defined in the partner agreement.
Do referral partners manage service delivery?
No. The iTrust team manages the platform demonstration, onboarding, testing engagement, and client delivery for referred opportunities.
Is technical expertise required?
Referral partners do not need technical delivery experience. Reseller and technology partners may require additional platform knowledge depending on their partnership model.
Can international organizations apply?
Yes. Applications are reviewed based on market alignment, regional support, and partnership fit.
Does submitting an application guarantee acceptance?
No. Each application is reviewed to confirm alignment between the partner, iTrust, and the clients being served.
